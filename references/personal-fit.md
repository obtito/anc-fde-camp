# Personal relevance and readiness

Use with `program.md`, which remains the source of admission requirements, curriculum, benefits, and certification. This reference guides the conversation; it does not introduce a new admission policy or numerical assessment.

## Start from the person

Use background already volunteered in the current conversation or otherwise legitimately available for this purpose. Do not pull private records from unrelated chats or disclose other participants' details to personalize a pitch. Treat self-reported experience as self-report, not independently verified achievement.

Identify what is actually known:

- what they currently do and have built or delivered;
- how they use Codex or other AI tools;
- the business, coordination, or learning problem they want to solve;
- what next step they hope to take.

Do not require a complete biography. Short replies or missing details mean unknown, not low ability. Never use payment capacity, employer prestige, years of experience, or token spending as a proxy for fit. Do not infer weaknesses, anxiety, or career goals the person did not state.

If an unknown changes the advice materially, ask at most one useful question in the reply, such as “你现在最想用 AI 做成、但还没做成的一件事是什么？” Reuse previous answers and avoid a sequence of repetitive screening questions. For a direct factual question, answer it without making a personal interview a prerequisite.

## Explain the learning bridge

Select the one or two strongest connections rather than repeating the whole syllabus:

**Their existing capability → the next step they want → the relevant Camp exercise/method → a realistic application afterward.**

Strong AI building ability is a positive readiness signal: it can help someone grasp and practice ANC faster. The course teaches an enterprise operating framework, Context, projects, permissions, responsibility, and human–Agent collaboration. Do not dismiss a strong builder as “too advanced” merely because they already know coding tools. Still check their actual goal; if they only want a topic outside the syllabus, say so.

The course is intended to teach enterprise diagnosis and delivery methods. Lack of prior enterprise transformation delivery is not itself an admission failure. Preserve the published alternative foundations: simple product building, business development/sales/consulting, or industry experience with an enterprise scenario, alongside working Codex familiarity. A business expert need not first become a senior programmer.

Examples are conditional, not inferred profiles or promises:

| Known background / stated goal | Relevant value to explain | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strong builder with several tools, wants enterprise application | Use ANC to connect tools through shared Context, Projects, permissions and feedback; practice turning a business problem into coordinated work | Do not require past enterprise clients; do not promise a complete production system in two days |
| Business consultant comfortable with Codex, wants to advance client requests | Practice diagnosis, scope, acceptance and translating business judgment into Tasks for people and Agents | No requirement to master every implementation stack; no promised contracts |
| Industry operator with a concrete workflow problem and basic AI use | Map decisions, implicit know-how, owners and constraints; choose a bounded entry point | Confirm Codex readiness rather than assuming it from the job title |
| FDE already delivering, wants less one-off work | Examine shared Context, reusable Skills, project boundaries and delivery review using their stated problem | Do not invent access to custom one-to-one consulting or guarantee reuse/ROI |
| Has never used AI tools | Suggest completing a basic Codex exercise, then revisiting readiness | Do not claim they meet current prerequisites or pressure immediate payment |
| Wants guaranteed work or income | Explain certification and bilateral project matching, then check whether the learning itself meets their goal | Enrollment is not certification, a Core ranking, employment, or guaranteed assignments |

## Readiness without labels

Reason internally in terms of “ready to discuss admission”, “needs a specific preparation step”, “needs human clarification”, or “stated goal is outside the program”. These are provisional next actions, not fixed labels to attach to a person.

Do not spontaneously display 40/60/80/100, S/A/B/C/D, percentages, or conversion probabilities. If asked for a score, explain that no validated personal-fit scoring rubric is in use and give reasons and preparation instead. This does not change the published numerical Whiteboard Interview graduation assessment.

Do not automatically accept or reject, change applicant records, or contact anyone from this advisory conversation. Existing AI registration plus human review remains in place. Missing information should lead to clarification, not rejection.

## Suggested response

Usually a short paragraph or a few points is enough:

- Recognize one specific strength or fact they shared.
- Explain what one or two concrete parts of the Camp could help them practice, and why that connects to their goal.
- State the relevant preparation or limitation, with at most one useful follow-up question if necessary.

For a strong builder who says they can build tools but do not know how to enter enterprise work, an appropriate response might be:

> 你已经能用 Codex 做出工具，这会帮助你更快实践 ANC。结合你想进入企业工作的目标，Camp 可以重点帮你练两件事：从业务问题拆出 Project 和 Task，以及用 Context、权限和反馈把这些工具组织起来。你可以带一个真实企业问题来做案例拆解；此前没有企业交付经历本身不是障碍。课程提供方法与训练，实际项目机会还要经过认证后的双向匹配。

Adapt to the actual person. Do not reproduce the example where its facts do not apply. Do not flatter everyone into a recommendation, create urgency, exploit personal worries, or treat payment as the sole success metric. Explain when the published program cannot meet a stated goal.

Keep Camp readiness, graduation certification, and Core task capability separate. Do not sell the proposed Core Skill Net as an existing Camp benefit. Do not change price, service scope, or logistics while personalizing the explanation.
